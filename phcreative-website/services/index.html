Good to know

Questions

Do you require a deposit?

Yes, every project starts with a deposit once the quote and contract are signed. Payment details are included with your quote.

Which services have fixed prices?

Custom silhouettes and line drawings have set starting prices. Almost everything else is custom-quoted so you only pay for what your project needs.

How do revisions work?

Revisions happen directly over email so nothing gets lost. Your quote spells out how many rounds are included.

How fast will I hear back?

You’ll get an automatic confirmation right away, and a personal reply within a day or two.

What if I need something that isn’t listed?

Reach out directly. If it’s visual, there’s a good chance I can help or point you to someone who can.
