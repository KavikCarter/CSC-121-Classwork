## 6. Potential Questions Answered

### Brand
1. What should someone feel when they land on PHC? They're in the right place to have their problems solved and visuals created.
2. What three words describe the brand? Unique. Exciting. Essential.
3. What do I absolutely NOT want the brand to feel like? Generic. Bland. Corporate.
4. What colors are mandatory? Coral. Navy blue more than black. Warm and cool color contrasts.
5. What colors should never dominate? Block and white
6. What fonts do I use? Playfair Display. Neue
7. What imagery represents PHC? Icons representing 3 C's of design: color (paint palette), concept, composition. Art tools like camera and slate (clapper). Fox. Fox using tail as paint brush.
8. How prominent should the fox concept be? Enough to be noticeable, but not over-powering.
9. How playful vs. sophisticated? 60% playful/unique, 40% sophisticated/classy.
10. How artistic vs. corporate? 75% artistic aesthetic, 25% corporate/professional.

### Audience
11. Who is my ideal client? Someone needing visual expertise to complete a project.
12. Who do I want more of? Those who respect the creative craft eager to connect and collaborate.
13. Who do I NOT want to attract? Those who don't communicate clearly and change their mind, constantly not being satisfied.
14. What problems am I solving? Clearly creating visuals fruition from ideas to final concept.
15. What makes someone choose me instead of a generic designer? All-around comprehension of creative process and multitude of possible solutions carried from start to finish.

### Services
16. What do I actually sell? Solutions
17. Which services have fixed prices? Line drawing and silhouettes
18. Which require custom quotes? Almost all
19. Which services require deposits? All
20. What information do I need before quoting each one? Scope. Timeline. Budget. Goal.

### Portfolio
21. What are my 10 strongest pieces? Film posters, branding designs, art gallery pieces and photography
22. What work makes me money? Commissioned film posters, logos and branding, event photography, camera crew film work
23. What work demonstrates where I am going? Film and graphics
24. What work should NOT be shown anymore? Generic family photo sessions
25. Which projects deserve their own case study? Intention behind art gallery pieces and behind finished design concepts for clients showcasing the care and research for each completed project.

### Sales
26. How do people contact me? Directly through email or inquiry. Possibly social media when relevant.

28. How quickly do I want to respond? Next day or couple days later
29. Where do I collect deposits? Venmo?
30. How do I handle revisions? Email, direct contact.
31. What happens if someone wants something outside my listed services? Contact directly.

### Shop
