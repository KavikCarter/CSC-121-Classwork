About

Hi, I’m Payton.

I’m a designer, photographer and film crew member, and the creative behind Payton Hood Creative.

I work with filmmakers, artists and small businesses who have an idea, a story or a brand that needs to become something people can see. Some days that’s a festival logo with a river hidden in the letterforms. Some days it’s a camera on my shoulder on set. Either way it’s the same job: taking an idea from concept to finished visual with care and research at every step.

Why PHC

Not a generic designer. A creative partner.

Because I work across design, photography and film, I understand the whole creative process: how a poster has to work with the trailer, how a brand has to live on a sign, a sticker and a screen. That means more possible solutions for you, and someone who carries your project from start to finish.

I love working with people who respect the craft and come ready to connect and collaborate.
