## 3. Brand direction (from her notes)

- **Three words:** Unique. Exciting. Essential.
- **Must not feel:** generic, bland, corporate.
- **Mix:** 60% playful/unique to 40% sophisticated; 75% artistic to 25% corporate.
- **How visitors should feel:** they're in the right place to have their problem solved and their visuals made.
- **Colors:** coral is mandatory. Use navy instead of black, and warm/cool contrast. **Black and white must never dominate.**
  - Palette: Peach Nectar #FFBB9F, Limpet Shell #96DCDF, Eclipse #3B3A50, Dress Blues #363D4E, Papaya Punch #FFA086, Bleached Aqua #AFDEDC, Clear Water #A8D5DF, Coral Reef #FCA481, Peach Fuzz #FFC195.
  - The site uses Dress Blues/Eclipse navy as the dark color, a peach-cream (#FFF4EC) instead of white, and coral accents.
- **Type:** a thick serif (**Playfair Display**) paired with a thin sans ("**Neue**", most likely Neue Montreal). Until the Neue files are licensed and added, the site falls back to Jost.
- **Textures and patterns:** ombre gradient fades, tapering stroke lines, and a navy background pattern that isn't too busy, with coral accents and type on top.
- **Custom pattern (built):** William Morris–inspired, with a crescent moon (Carolina), spade heart, fox-tail swirl, four-point star, feather (nature/Robin Hood) and taper lines. It's `assets/img/pattern.svg`.
- **Business-card tile (built):** an aqua/coral/sand floral tile, used as a band above the footer. It's `assets/img/tile-floral.svg`.
- **Logo:** her **PH monogram**, a coral circle with a cream ring and thin slanted P and H stems. The full version adds "Payton Hood" and "Creative" curved inside the ring. Both are redrawn as SVG in `assets/img/ph-monogram.svg` and `ph-logo-full.svg`. Replace them with her original logo file when it's available.
- **Imagery:** the 3 C's of design (Color as a paint palette, Concept, Composition), art tools such as a camera and a slate, and a **fox**, including a fox using its tail as a paintbrush. The fox should be noticeable but not overpowering.

## 7. How to add a new piece

**1. Prepare the image.** Make a web-sized JPEG, about 2000 px on the long edge at roughly 85% quality, and name it after the id. Example: `assets/img/work/carolina-sundancers-promo.jpg`. For a multi-page PDF, render page 1 (or the strongest page) to a JPEG.

**2. Add an entry to `window.PHC_PROJECTS` in `assets/js/projects.js`.** The order of the list is the display order.

```js
{
  id: "carolina-sundancers-promo",        // lowercase-hyphen, unique; also the image file name
  title: "Carolina Sundancers",            // what the client or project is called
  subtitle: "Festival social graphics",    // the type of work
  client: "Carolina Sundancers · Spartanburg, SC",
  year: "2025",                            // optional
  categories: ["branding", "film"],        // from: branding, film, photography, illustration, fineart
  src: "assets/img/work/carolina-sundancers-promo.jpg",
  size: "wide",                            // optional: "wide" spans 2 columns (landscape boards); omit for normal
  featured: true,                          // optional: shows on homepage/TRIFF
  services: ["Social graphics", "Typography"],
  description: "One or two sentences on what it is and the idea behind it, in Payton's voice (first person is fine).",
  points: ["Optional bullet on a design decision", "Another"]   // optional, shown in the project popup
}
```

**3. Optional extra placements:**
- To feature a piece in the **homepage carousel**, add `{ id: "...", label: "Short caption" }` to `window.PHC_HERO`. Add `contain: true` for posters and boards that shouldn't be cropped.
- To feature a piece in the **client spotlight**, add its id to `window.PHC_SPOTLIGHT`. It rotates with Fantascape, Reedy Reels, BCHS and King Wings.

**Writing style for descriptions:** plain, specific and warm. Name the real client and place, and describe what's actually in the image and the idea behind it. No filler, and don't invent facts (dates, awards, results) that aren't visible or given.

## 9. Launch checklist

1. Create an empty `phcreative-website` repo and move the site into it.
2. Deploy on Netlify or Cloudflare Pages.
3. Point GoDaddy DNS at the host. Edit the `A @` record and the `CNAME www` record only, and leave the MX (email) records alone.
4. Submit one test inquiry, then click the FormSubmit activation email.
5. Test the TRIFF QR code on a phone.
6. Optionally, add a GoatCounter analytics code in `assets/js/site.js`.
